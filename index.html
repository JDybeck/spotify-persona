Get your data

  1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy and log in.
  2. Request Extended streaming history for the full picture (all years, skips, shuffle). It takes up to 30 days.
  3. The faster Account data export also works, but only covers the last year and has no skip data.
  4. When Spotify emails you, download the zip and drop it above. No need to unzip.
